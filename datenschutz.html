3. Hosting (GitHub Pages)

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. The provider is GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you visit the site, the provider processes technically necessary data, in particular your IP address, the date and time of the request, the page requested and browser and system information (server log files). We have no access to this data.

The legal basis is our legitimate interest in providing the website securely and efficiently (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Data may be transferred to the USA on the basis of the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the EU Commission's standard contractual clauses. More information: GitHub Privacy Statement.

4. Fonts

The fonts used on this website are hosted locally. No connection to font providers such as Google Fonts is made.

5. Consent banner and Google Analytics

On your first visit, a banner asks whether we may use Google Analytics. Without your consent, Google Analytics is not loaded and no connection to Google's servers is made. We store your choice in your browser (Local Storage, entry “3xwu-consent-v1”) so we do not have to ask on every visit. The legal basis is § 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG in conjunction with Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. You can change your choice at any time via “Cookie settings” in the footer.

If you consent, we use Google Analytics 4, a web analytics service of Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. Google Analytics sets cookies (e.g. “_ga” and “_ga_…”) and processes data such as pages viewed, time on site, browser and device information and approximate location (country/region) to evaluate how our website is used. Google Analytics 4 does not store your IP address. The cookies last up to two years. Usage data is deleted after the retention period set in Google Analytics (14 months at most). Data may be transferred to Google LLC in the USA on the basis of the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 25(1) TDDDG). You can withdraw it at any time with effect for the future via “Cookie settings” in the footer. The Google Analytics cookies are then deleted. More information: Google Privacy Policy.

6. Contact form

If you use our contact form, we process your details (name, email address, message) and technical data such as your IP address and approximate location in order to answer your enquiry. The form is operated through the service Formspark (Trampoline Software SRL, Rue de Marsannay-la-Côte 16, 5032 Mazy, Belgium), which forwards your message to us. Formspark acts as our processor under Art. 28 GDPR; a data processing agreement is in place. According to the provider, data is stored within the European Economic Area. IP address and approximate location are kept there for up to 12 months. No scripts from the provider are loaded when you visit the page. Data is only transmitted once you submit the form.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (pre-contractual measures) or Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (answering enquiries). We delete your enquiry once it has been dealt with and no statutory retention duties apply.

7. Contact by email

If you contact us by email, we process the details you send (email address, name, content of your message) to handle your enquiry. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (pre-contractual measures) or Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (answering enquiries). We delete the data once it is no longer needed for that purpose and no statutory retention duties apply.

8. YouTube videos

We embed YouTube videos. The provider is Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. Videos are only loaded after you actively click on them. The legal basis is your consent given by clicking (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 25(1) TDDDG). You can withdraw it at any time with effect for the future by reloading the page and not playing the video. More information: Google Privacy Policy.
